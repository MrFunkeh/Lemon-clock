# Tools

- Soldering iron
- Solder
- Philips screw driver
- 3D printer
- Fillament (about 0.5KG)

# What is what

<img width="334" height="604" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cda91039-39b7-4e2b-a101-a5610426e67f" />

- The triangles in between two holes is a refference to where you can put zip ties for cable management.
- The little rectangle lines beside the snowman hole is the center point refference for the mounting screw.
- All of the other holes are most likely for use of screws to the other side or view ports.
- Rectangles in the corners are add on pads to make room for the screw heads, makes it rest flush on the wall.

# Printing

- To print it download the CAD files and  print it in the strongest orientation possible.
  (whatch this video for a guid on how to print in the correct orintation: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7aJ4dDyyf20)
- Tinker with the wall thicknesses and density as you like, make small non structural parts a light 20% infil and motor mounts and such like 75% infill.
- Add a brim or raft if neccessary to avoid print failures, add support for large overhangs or angled prints.
- Make sure to double check your slicer peramiters before printing and clean your build plate well.
